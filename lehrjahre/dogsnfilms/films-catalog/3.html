Summary:
"Casablanca," directed by Michael Curtiz, is a legendary romantic drama set against the backdrop of World War II. Starring Humphrey Bogart and Ingrid Bergman, the film is renowned for its captivating narrative, memorable dialogue, and timeless themes of love and sacrifice. The story unfolds in the titular city of Casablanca, blending romance, intrigue, and political turmoil in a masterfully crafted cinematic experience.
